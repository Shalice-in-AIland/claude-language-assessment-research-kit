# Citation-integrity self-check — a deliberately flawed sample

> ⚠️ **Every reference in this file is either invented or planted with a deliberate error. Do not cite anything from this file, and do not let it into a real bibliography or reference manager.** It exists only so you can watch the portable citation check work.

## How to use it (2 minutes)

1. Open a new chat in any AI assistant that can search the web.
2. Paste in [`citation-integrity-portable.md`](citation-integrity-portable.md) (or attach it).
3. Paste **the passage and reference list below**.
4. Say: *"Follow the citation-integrity instructions above: check the citations in this passage against the reference list."*
5. Compare what comes back against [`self-check-answer-key.md`](self-check-answer-key.md).

You should see a verdict banner, a short list of things to fix (grouped by urgency), and — importantly — some items parked as *"couldn't verify — your call"* rather than declared fake. That honest three-way split (verified / to-fix / your-call) is the behaviour to look for.

## The passage

Large language models are now routine tools in automated writing evaluation, and early work showed AI scoring of learner essays reaching useful reliability (Mizumoto & Eguchi, 2023). Cross-model comparisons have since broadened the evidence base (Okonkwo & Reyes, 2024), and trait-level analyses report that "AI raters now agree with expert humans across every analytic dimension" (Larsson & Mehta, 2026), a finding increasingly treated as settled in the applied-linguistics literature.

Bias, however, remains a live concern. Analytic-dimension scoring is still harder than holistic scoring for current systems (Bannò et al., 2024), and rater-training effects that were first described for human raters (Lumley, 2005) appear to carry over to AI raters as well. A recent multi-site study found that model–human agreement drops sharply for lower-proficiency writers (Adeyemi, 2025), echoing a broader pattern documented across the automated-scoring literature (Petrov, 2023).
