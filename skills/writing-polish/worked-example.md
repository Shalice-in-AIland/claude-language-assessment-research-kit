## The section to polish

> In this study, a lot of attention was paid to rater consistency. The raters was trained in two sessions, and the analysis of the scores was conducted using many-facet Rasch measurement [@doe2019]. Inter-rater agreement was acceptable (κ = .71, n = 48 essays). Because the model received the same rubric, exemplars and scoring instructions as the human raters, any difference between their scores reflects how each applied the rubric rather than what each was shown, and that is the comparison the design was built to make. The model overestimated scores in low proficiency. This proves that the model cannot replace human raters, and Roe clearly failed to notice this problem [@roe2023].
